# CPOS Recommendation / Approval / Award Capability v1.0

## User meaning

Turn a frozen comparison basis into a governed buying decision while keeping recommendation, approval, award and commitment distinct.

## Recommendation

- sourcing event and frozen ComparisonSnapshot;
- source ProcurementRouteDecision and policy version;
- frozen/current TechnicalEvaluation basis where applicable;
- recommended supplier(s) and selected revision/confirmed basis;
- recommended value/currency;
- exact ProcurementBudgetBasis/version and variance where available;
- estimating handover allowance/reference where relevant and clearly distinguished from live budget authority;
- technical/commercial rationale;
- unresolved/resolved TechnicalApprovalDependency state and conditions;
- deviations/exclusions/risks;
- negotiated improvements;
- supplier registration/qualification/compliance state relied upon;
- supplier performance/workload/exposure context relevant to the decision;
- competitive-coverage result and any route-policy exception;
- non-lowest-bid justification;
- split-award/sole-source rationale;
- supporting source documents/correspondence;
- preparer/date.

## Approval Case

- approval policy/DOA basis;
- procurement-route/exception policy basis;
- requested approvers and current ball-in-court;
- amount/scope thresholds;
- required evidence/justifications;
- technical-evaluation/qualification/approval conditions required by policy;
- approve/reject/return-for-revision actions;
- comments/conditions;
- delegated-authority provenance;
- immutable action history.

Approval revalidates current truth and authority at transition time. A stale budget, expired qualification, failed competition requirement, changed technical evaluation or newly failed mandatory gate cannot be hidden merely because the recommendation was prepared earlier.

## Award Decision

Award records the approved supplier/basis/scope and decision time. It does not itself create a legally effective PO/subcontract. Award may be full, split, conditional or no-award. Any divergence from the approved recommendation is explicit and governed.

Historical award provenance preserves the exact comparison snapshot, technical-evaluation basis, budget basis, route/policy decision, supplier qualification/compliance facts and technical-approval state used by the decision.

## Outputs / register

Recommendation PDF, approval trail/export, award decision record and a register showing number, project, event, supplier, recommended value, status, pending approver, age, budget variance, competition/policy exception, technical/qualification/risk flags and order-conversion status.

## AI assist

AI may draft recommendation narratives, summarize deviations, benchmark prices and surface supplier/schedule risks using cited deterministic data. AI cannot approve, award, fabricate budget/qualification/technical/policy facts, convert proposed technical analysis into approval, infer execution or hide contrary evidence.

## Acceptance

A buyer recommends a supplier who is not the lowest bidder, cites the exact comparison/technical-evaluation/budget/procurement-policy versions, explains commercial/technical and supplier-capacity reasons, handles an under-competition/sole-source exception through the required justification and DOA, receives conditional approval subject to one external technical requirement, later satisfies/revalidates that gate, records the award and converts only the approved basis into LPO/PO/Subcontract without changing the original comparison.
