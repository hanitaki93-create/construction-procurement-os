# Owner / Domain Meaning Decision — Architecture V2

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Decision:** `MEANING_PASS`
**Scope:** Architecture V2 build-relevant capabilities R01–R10 plus cross-cutting product quality/accessibility/localization/recovery.

## Decision basis

The project owner rejected the old B04–B06 product increment because it was not a credible procurement product despite technically strong substrate. Architecture V2 was therefore rebuilt from accepted B03 with a capability-first product layer and independently hostile-audited.

The external Claude audit returned FAIL only on three surgical freeze blockers:
1. missing Standard §C field-contract detail;
2. missing concrete document-class numbering policies;
3. incomplete manifest/CAP-042 quality binding.

The same audit explicitly judged the underlying product architecture sound: no orphan Phase-1 SPINE area, no DI orphan, no second XL gravity well, ERP/CDE and AI boundaries sufficient, specialist construction-procurement differentiation genuine, and scenarios B–H passing. Scenario A failed only because of field/numbering detail.

The owner then explicitly authorized this rule for the final external-audit cycle:

> If the comments are simple and closable, treat the corrected architecture as passed, freeze it, and prepare to start coding rather than consuming another audit cycle.

The closure work remained exactly within those surgical findings plus the audit's four low-cost amendments. `POST_CLAUDE_BLOCKER_CLOSURE_VERIFICATION_V1_0.md` records the correction evidence. No new architecture contradiction or gravity-well change was discovered.

## Domain acceptance

The product meaning now accepted for direct build includes:
- serious supplier/subcontractor master, registration, qualification, compliance, contextual eligibility and performance/exposure;
- optional item/material/service/scope master plus free-form construction procurement;
- governed UOM/cost/WBS/budget/reference data;
- human Material/Purchase Requisition with lines, distributions, approvals and controlled routing;
- Procurement Package + Scope Library + estimating handover + early procurement schedule;
- route/competition/direct/sole-source policy with explicit exception/approval evidence;
- RFQ/Tender formed from approved demand without re-keying, professional issue artifacts, addenda and low-friction supplier access;
- supplier quotations/revisions preserving exact source truth;
- formal technical tender evaluation separated from later material/submittal/CDE approval;
- source/normalized/buyer-adjusted/supplier-confirmed commercial layers;
- source-cited bounded AI extraction with human confirmation;
- recommendation, DOA approval and AwardDecision distinct from commitment;
- early LPO/PO/Subcontract formation, professional issued document and execution/acknowledgment/eSignature state;
- demand/scope conservation so competing transactions cannot double-commit approved authority;
- procurement workbench/registers/schedule/analytics and receipt/GRN/ERP seam;
- WCAG 2.2 AA target, responsive web, Arabic/RTL and safe business-effect recovery/error semantics;
- Oracle/SAP/CMiC/Vista/Aconex/etc. coexistence through explicit OWN/MIRROR/REFERENCE authority rather than duplicate editable ledgers.

## Build-authority distinction

`MEANING_PASS` authorizes the build-relevant Architecture V2 product meaning. It does not promote retained R12+ deep commercial capabilities into the first build or pretend they have first-spine field detail. Framework/deep commercial/claims/valuation/retention/advance/recovery/closeout remain architecturally preserved and require their own capability-detail closure before their implementation session.

## Governance after freeze

After Architecture V2 freeze:
- the frozen architecture + capability specs + field contracts are direct implementation authority;
- R labels are thin sequencing aids only;
- focused multi-hour vertical build/test/debug sessions are preferred;
- no successor-prompt or interpretation bureaucracy may redefine frozen product meaning;
- new architecture paperwork is required only when implementation discovers a genuine contradiction/change to frozen meaning;
- technical tests/hostile audits remain necessary for code/invariants, but they do not reopen already accepted product meaning without evidence.

**OWNER_DOMAIN_DECISION = MEANING_PASS**
