# CPOS Rebuilt Block Program v0.1

**Status:** REBASELINE CANDIDATE / OLD B07-B18 SUPERSEDED

The rebuilt program is capability-first. Every block requires accepted CapabilitySpecifications, `MEANING_PASS`, real UI, professional outputs where applicable, user-language acceptance scenarios, technical verification and hostile audit for load-bearing semantics.

## R00 — Rebaseline / capability compiler

- complete retained-capability inventory from Phase-1 scope and renewed competitor evidence;
- implement capability-completeness compiler/check;
- freeze user vocabulary and product navigation model;
- freeze salvage/replace decisions;
- no transaction-domain coding until R00 PASS.

## R01 — Shared master/reference, numbering and document foundation

- UOM registry;
- item/material/service/scope master with free-form escape;
- cost/WBS references and project delivery locations;
- currencies/tax/payment-term references;
- governed numbering service and document-class policies;
- product-owned versioned template/rendering foundation;
- actual admin/user surfaces for maintaining permitted reference data.

## R02 — Supplier/Subcontractor Master + Compliance

- serious supplier profile;
- multiple contacts/addresses;
- legal/trading identifiers, TRN/VAT and licence/registration records;
- categories/trades;
- time-bounded compliance documents and eligibility;
- supplier history/register/search;
- evidence attachments behind ordinary document UX.

## R03 — Material/Purchase Requisition

- MR/PR headers, lines and distributions;
- catalogue and free-form lines;
- approval/review routing;
- requested/approved/sourced/ordered/received positions;
- attachments and MR PDF/export;
- route approved lines to RFQ, package or direct-order disposition;
- map to/salvage RequirementAllocation only where semantically valid.

## R04 — RFQ/Tender Formation and Issue

- create RFQ from approved MR/package lines without re-entry;
- supplier selection using master/compliance context;
- real pricing/bid-form structure;
- instructions, commercial terms, due dates, documents and addenda;
- governed RFQ numbering;
- branded PDF/Excel issue package;
- manual download/send path plus secure participation substrate;
- salvage strong B06 version/issue/grant semantics.

## R05 — Supplier Participation, Quotations and Revisions

- secure link/task response;
- file/email/manual/buyer-on-behalf capture;
- intent/no-bid;
- original supplier source files preserved;
- revision/withdrawal/late response lifecycle;
- structured price/lead-time/validity/terms where supplied;
- response register and supplier-visible receipt/status.

## R06 — Bid Comparison / Leveling + bounded AI extraction

- deterministic side-by-side comparison;
- source vs normalized vs buyer-adjusted vs supplier-confirmed layers;
- missing/excluded/alternate/substitute/bundled/partial mapping;
- technical/commercial deviations and clarifications;
- AI extraction from PDF/Excel with exact source citation, confidence and human confirmation;
- comparison workbook/PDF/export;
- frozen ComparisonSnapshot.

## R07 — Negotiation, Recommendation, Approval and Award

- negotiation/clarification outcome capture;
- recommendation with preferred supplier, value, budget/cost context and justification;
- DOA/approval case;
- non-lowest, split and sole-source handling;
- AwardDecision distinct from commitment;
- recommendation/approval documents and decision register.

## R08 — Early LPO/PO/Subcontract Formation

- convert awarded/confirmed basis without re-keying;
- LPO/PO/Subcontract header + lines/SOV;
- payment, delivery, tax, currency and standard terms;
- governed numbering;
- approvals/signatory controls;
- professional issue-ready PDF and attachment annexures;
- revision/amendment boundary;
- ERP/export/handoff seam.

## R09 — Procurement Workbench / Schedule / Registers

- role-focused home/work queue;
- MR, RFQ, response, comparison, recommendation, award and order registers;
- procurement schedule with required/baseline/forecast/supplier-confirmed dates;
- actual milestones from domain events;
- overdue/risk/ball-in-court indicators;
- search/filter/export/drill-down;
- responsive/RTL/accessibility consolidation.

## R10 — Receipt / GRN / Downstream Reconciliation

- delivery vs receipt vs acceptance distinctions;
- partial receipt, damage/shortage/return evidence;
- GRN/receipt document where owned;
- PO balance visibility;
- invoice/AP/ERP reference and reconciliation seams;
- no full warehouse/GL duplication.

## R11 — Production hardening and pilot

- permission/security hardening;
- reliability/performance/observability;
- migration/import;
- configuration and template validation;
- real contractor pilot and measured workflow acceptance;
- no commercial claim without observed evidence.

## R12+ — Deep commercial administration and advanced intelligence

After the first procurement spine is proven, reopen the deeper P07 work: commitment changes, variations, advances, retention, claims, certification, recovery, closeout/security/warranty. Expand AI into historical price intelligence, supplier performance, procurement risk, recommendation briefing and controlled automation only on proven deterministic data.

## Non-negotiable sequencing change

There is no later 'build the real web' block. Every R01-R10 block owns the real surface required to operate that capability. A technically correct hidden object is not block completion.
