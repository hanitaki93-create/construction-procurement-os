# CPOS ERP-Coexistence Product Bar v0.1

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Status:** PRODUCT POSITIONING / ARCHITECTURE INPUT

## Target

CPOS must be valuable to a contractor that already owns Oracle, SAP, CMiC, Vista, Dynamics or another ERP.

Therefore CPOS is not justified by merely reproducing requisition, PO and supplier tables. Those are the minimum product floor.

## CPOS should own or excel at

- construction-specific MR/package demand intake;
- supplier/subcontractor sourcing context and compliance;
- tender/RFQ formation and supplier participation;
- quotation/revision source truth;
- document ingestion and evidence provenance;
- bid normalization/leveling and scope-gap intelligence;
- recommendation/approval/award evidence;
- rapid LPO/PO/subcontract formation from approved award basis;
- procurement schedule/long-lead control;
- supplier history/performance/capacity context;
- professional procurement documents and registers;
- AI-assisted extraction, analysis, drafting and exception detection with source citation;
- cross-project procurement intelligence.

## ERP may remain authoritative for

Depending on tenant configuration/integration contract:
- GL/accounting posting;
- AP/invoice authority;
- payment execution;
- enterprise budget/job-cost source;
- inventory/warehouse ledger;
- tax master authority;
- financial vendor master fields that CPOS mirrors rather than owns.

CPOS must preserve OWN/MIRROR/REFERENCE authority per field/event and show synchronization/reconciliation state rather than create a second editable ledger.

## Product test

For every planned capability ask:

> If a contractor already has a functioning ERP, does CPOS make the procurement team materially faster, more informed, easier to collaborate with, more auditable, or more automated?

If the answer is only 'CPOS stores the same field too', the capability is not differentiation; it is either necessary compatibility floor or unjustified duplication.

## AI test

AI must exploit procurement-specific context that generic ERP workflows do not expose well: source documents, line/scope identity, historical quotes, commercial deviations, supplier compliance/performance, schedule urgency and award rationale.

This is the route to an ERP-adjacent system of action rather than a weak ERP replacement.
