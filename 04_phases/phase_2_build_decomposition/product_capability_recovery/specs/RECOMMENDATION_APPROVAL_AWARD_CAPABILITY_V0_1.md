# CPOS Recommendation / Approval / Award Capability v0.1

**Status:** DRAFT / R00 MEANING REVIEW REQUIRED

## User meaning

The system must turn a frozen comparison basis into a governed buying decision while keeping recommendation, approval, award and commitment distinct.

## Recommendation

Fields include:
- sourcing event and frozen ComparisonSnapshot;
- recommended supplier(s) and revision/basis;
- recommended value/currency;
- budget/cost-code context and variance where available;
- commercial/technical rationale;
- major deviations/exclusions/risks;
- negotiated improvements;
- non-lowest-bid justification where applicable;
- split-award/sole-source rationale;
- attachments and supporting evidence;
- preparer and date.

## Approval Case

- approval policy/DOA basis;
- requested approvers and current ball-in-court;
- amount/scope thresholds;
- required evidence/justifications;
- approve/reject/return-for-revision actions;
- comments and conditions;
- delegated authority provenance;
- immutable action history.

Approval must revalidate current truth and authority at transition time.

## Award Decision

Award records the approved supplier/basis/scope and decision time. It does not itself create a legal/effective PO/subcontract. Award may be full, split or no-award. Any divergence from the approved recommendation is explicit and governed.

## Documents / outputs

- recommendation document/PDF;
- approval trail/export;
- award notice/internal decision record;
- optional supplier award/regret communication template later.

## Register / workbench

Recommendation/approval register shows number, project, event, supplier, recommended value, status, pending approver, age, variance, risk flags and award status.

## AI assist

AI may draft recommendation narratives, summarize deviations, benchmark prices and surface risks using cited deterministic data. AI cannot approve, award, fabricate budget facts or hide contrary comparison evidence.

## Acceptance

A buyer can recommend a supplier who is not the lowest bidder, explain the commercial/technical reason, route the case through DOA, receive conditional approval, record the award, and convert only the approved award basis into LPO/PO/Subcontract without changing the original comparison.
