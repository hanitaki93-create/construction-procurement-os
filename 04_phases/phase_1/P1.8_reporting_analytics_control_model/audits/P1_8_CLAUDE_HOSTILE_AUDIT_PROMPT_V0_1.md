5. whether generic “actual,” “cost,” “current,” “overdue,” “period” or “all” meanings are impossible, and whether physical, commercial/certified, accounting-posted and paid/cash actuals remain separate;
6. whether source/effective/recorded/observed/known-at/as-of/execution/publication times are sufficiently closed for historical and current reporting;

10. whether `ResultQualityVector` preserves all independent dimensions and `DecisionUseAssessment` prevents a stale/partial/reconciliation-open result from being called complete or fit for approval;
11. whether `ReportDecisionUseProfile` and `ReportUseAssessment` prevent informational or monitoring metrics from becoming approval/external-issue support merely through report composition or title;
12. whether `IssueTimeReportUseAssessment` and `SubsequentRelianceAssessment` correctly preserve historical issue validity while rechecking current access, policy, evidence, restatement and intended use for a new decision;

14. whether ratios, rates, averages, percentiles and distinct counts are recomputed correctly rather than summed or averaged naively;
15. whether unlike actual families, mixed time cuts, incompatible FX purposes, calendars, populations or quality states are segmented, limited or blocked rather than forced into a total;

17. whether supplier-performance analytics remains tenant-private, dimension-specific and unable to create global reputation, debarment or award authority;
18. whether control observations remain derived attention conditions and cannot become business state, workflow truth, no-effect proof or a general GRC/CPM/BPM subsystem;
19. whether report/export/evidence semantics keep live views, snapshots, PDF/XLSX/CSV artifacts, issue, dispatch, delivery and acknowledgment distinct;
20. whether edited workbooks or imported report files can bypass bounded source/domain correction;
21. whether query/chat answers must use registered operations, exact result classes, citations and quality disclosure—and cannot claim all from partial, guess actual family, assert causation or use session memory/old reports as authority;
22. whether the A0–A3 minimum report pack truly works end to end with manual/structured evidence and no connector, public API, broker, chat, AI, warehouse, P07 or migration;
