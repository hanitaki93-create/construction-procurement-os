# 3. Blocker closure

## BL-P18-01 — CLOSED

Occurrence, lineage, conservation and metric-specific contribution identity are distinct.

`ContributionDispositionRule` closes signed inclusion, reversal, replacement, reclassification, proportional allocation, duplicate replay and unsupported correction behavior.

Reverse-and-replace +100 / -100 / +120 yields:

- current position 120 under the exact as-of rule;
- period flows according to effective/recorded occurrence times;
- all occurrences retained;
- no lineage dedupe or double count.

## BL-P18-02 — CLOSED

Quality is a complete `ResultQualityVector`, not one mutually exclusive label.

Every decision use has exact permitted/limited/blocked assessment.

A result may be stale, partial, reconciliation-open and indeterminate-excluded simultaneously without one dimension suppressing another.

Presentation summary is derived per use and cannot upgrade or hide the vector.

## BL-P18-03 — CLOSED

Every ReportDefinition binds explicit use profiles.

Every ReportExecution/Snapshot proves required-member use eligibility, quality, evidence, access and issue fitness.

Informational/monitoring metrics cannot become approval support through title, composition or warnings.

## Subsequent reliance gap — CLOSED

Original issue-time assessment remains historical.

Any new load-bearing use of an old report revalidates current access, policy, restatement, reconstruction and intended use through `SubsequentRelianceAssessment`.

Later blocking does not rewrite old issue; old permission does not grant perpetual/current reliance.
