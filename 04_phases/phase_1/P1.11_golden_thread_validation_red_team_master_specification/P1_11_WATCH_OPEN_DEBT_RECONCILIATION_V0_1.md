# 1. Classification vocabulary

Every prior watch/debt is exactly one:

- `CLOSED_IN_FROZEN_SEMANTICS` — semantic clause is incorporated into a controlling frozen contract.
- `INHERITED_BUILD_CONSTRAINT` — architecture meaning is closed; physical implementation/test must prove it.
- `MANDATORY_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_GATE` — architecture supplies the hypothesis/measurement, but contractor/supplier/prototype/pilot evidence is still required.
- `LEGAL_OR_JURISDICTIONAL_EVIDENCE_DEBT` — cannot be asserted without applicable legal/contractual evidence.
- `NON_SPINE_PHYSICAL_DEFERRAL` — physical choice is intentionally open with no SPINE semantic ambiguity.
- `BLOCKED_ARCHITECTURE_GAP` — would block Phase 1 closure.

Current blocked gaps: **NONE claimed; subject to P1.11 no-invention audit.**
