## WP-0 — Control baseline

- verify P1.0–P1.10 closure/freeze state;
- synchronize ADR log and phase reconciliation;
- establish precedence and evidence authority;
- prohibit implementation and scope invention.

## WP-1 — Architecture catalogue and traceability index

- canonical phase/file index;
- subject/fact/event/operation/report/interaction/NFR/AI registers;
- requirement ↔ evidence ↔ ADR ↔ frozen clause ↔ golden-thread mapping;
- orphan and conflict detection.

## WP-2 — Watch/open-debt reconciliation

- gather every Claude/internal watch from P1.4–P1.10;
- classify CLOSED / INHERITED_BUILD_CONSTRAINT / VALIDATION_GATE / LEGAL_EVIDENCE_DEBT / NON-SPINE_DEFERRAL;
- preserve FT-02, FT-06, FT-09/CR-02, FT-10, ADR-0010 and ADR-0011;
- prove no hidden architecture choice remains.

## WP-3 — Golden-thread atlas

Define at least twenty threads with:

- preconditions and actors;
- canonical entities/facts;
- operations/states/effects/events;
- evidence/communication/integration;
- reports/interactions;
- failures/corrections/NFR/AI-off paths;
- expected end position;
- architecture-question register.

## WP-4 — A0–A3 no-dependency proof

Execute ordinary material, competitive tender, supplier revision, approval, AwardDecision, manual handoff, reporting and recovery without:

- P07;
- named connectors;
- persistent supplier account/network;
- chat/AI;
- warehouse/BI.

## WP-5 — P07 and closed-period proof

Execute Commitment, change, valuation, certification, retention/advance/recovery, reclassification, closed-period correction and external accounting reconciliation under one effect algebra.

## WP-6 — Evidence/integration/migration proof

Execute issue/delivery/acknowledgment, effect-indeterminate connector call, migration with incomplete history, untrusted import and evidence disposition/restore.

## WP-7 — Reporting/UX proof

Execute subset/range/restricted/stale/restated report cases and verify identical meaning through screen, export and optional chat, including subsequent reliance.

## WP-8 — NFR/AI proof

Execute outage/restore, backpressure, provider disappearance, capability configuration monotonicity, incomplete AI context, stale proposal, L5 confirmation and AI-off fallback.

## WP-9 — Artifact completeness audit

Test:

- entity/fact ownership;
- lifecycle/state/guard/effect/correction completeness;
- operation/UI/API ownership;
- report derivability;
- integration/migration/failure coverage;
- NFR measurability;
- AI optionality;
- requirements traceability.

## WP-10 — Master specification candidate

Assemble:

- executive architecture contract;
- build-facing maps;
- precedence and frozen references;
- golden-thread atlas;
- traceability and deferrals;
- physical-decision boundaries;
- Phase 2 entry contract.

## WP-11 — Internal hostile/no-invention audit

A fresh-review simulation attacks:

- missing members and authorship boundaries;
- cross-phase contradictions;
- hidden truth writers;
- unowned action/screen/report/API;
- recovery and correction gaps;
- second-XL growth;
- false validation claims.

Record FAIL, remediate narrowly, then rerun the complete matrix.

## WP-12 — Independent audit packet

Create a self-contained packet that allows an independent reviewer to execute at least two complex golden threads and answer exact closure gates without repository access.

## WP-13 — Final closure after dual PASS

Only after internal and Claude PASS:

- issue frozen Phase 1 master specification;
- final ADR/watch/open-debt reconciliation;
- final Phase 1 verdict/checkpoint;
- set architecture COMPLETE / INTERNALLY VALIDATED;
- keep external/product/commercial validation explicitly pending;
- unlock Phase 2 build decomposition only, not implementation unless separately authorized.

## Execution rule

Work packages may be consolidated for speed, but no gate may be skipped. A discovered architecture question blocks closure until remediated or formally deferred outside V1 SPINE with demonstrated non-impact.
