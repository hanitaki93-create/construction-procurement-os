# 3. Blind execution A — GT-10 communication-gated effect

## Given

An issued pre-effective basis requires governed communication satisfaction before a domain effect. Provider callbacks can arrive late, concurrently or be corrected later. The owning domain may cancel before a future offset completes.

## Questions and controlling answers

### What is the authoritative issue basis?

The immutable issued artifact/member set, addressee/channel basis, CommunicationSatisfactionRule and exact calendar/configuration versions under P1.6.

### Do delivery/read/ack facts imply one another?

No. They are separate CommunicationOccurrences. The frozen rule explicitly names required supporting facts.

### What makes the condition satisfied?

First accepted evaluation of the frozen rule creates one canonical `CommunicationSatisfactionSnapshot`; concurrency binds one canonical snapshot/composite identity.

### Does that snapshot create business truth?

No. It is evidence/control history. A separate registered owning-domain command validates current lifecycle/authority and establishes one immutable domain event/effect.

### What if the owning domain cancelled before establishment?

The domain lifecycle disposition controls whether establishment remains permitted. Evidence recomputation does not override it.

### What if the provider retracts the callback later?

The retraction becomes contradictory/corrective evidence. It does not silently remove or retime the established domain effect. Any consequence change uses an owning-domain correction command.

### What if the request times out?

Timeout/absence does not prove no effect. The operation/result uses the P1.7 effect taxonomy and continuation/reconciliation rules.

### What surface/API owns the actions?

Communication issue/history surfaces and owning-domain establishment/recovery surfaces; exact actions are registered COMMAND/ASYNC_OPERATION/QUERY operations. No evidence callback writes the domain directly.

### Architecture questions required

**0.**

# 4. Blind execution B — GT-12 connector timeout after possible external effect

## Given

An effect-bearing publication is sent to an external system. The connection times out after the effect boundary may have been crossed. A callback arrives uncorrelated and adapter conformance later degrades.

## Questions and controlling answers

### What identities exist before send?

LogicalCommandId, invocation/async identity, immutable PublicationIntent, representation/mapping/disclosure versions, exact target basis, idempotency and continuation identities.

### Which stage follows timeout?

`EFFECT_INDETERMINATE` unless positive evidence proves pre-effect or terminal no-effect. Missing callback/absence/current-only lookup cannot supply that proof.

### May UI, connector or agent retry?

No ordinary retry, rebind, cancellation-as-resolution or conflicting replacement while indeterminate. Permitted actions are lookup, reconciliation, evidence capture, manual/block disposition and explanation.

### What happens to the uncorrelated callback?

It becomes a quarantined ExternalObservation with typed authenticity/validation class and retention/security basis. It may later correlate; it cannot directly create domain truth.

### What if effect becomes confirmed?

Stage advances immutably to the exact external/domain effect stage and produces the owning result/event under the registered operation.

### What if it can never be resolved?

An authorized accepted-unresolved-external-position disposition may close the operational reconciliation item without asserting no effect or permitting unsafe replay. Historical effect uncertainty remains.

### Does later adapter nonconformance rewrite history?

No. It changes current/future eligibility and confidence only. Historical intent, observations and effects retain original lineage.

# 5. Blind execution C — GT-13 closed-period certified error correction

## Given

A certified commercial value in a closed period is later proven wrong. The original report was externally issued, and ERP posting/payment records exist separately.

## Questions and controlling answers

### Can the original certificate/effect be edited?

No. Commercial effects and issued evidence/report snapshots are immutable.

### Which correction occurs?

A product-owned policy and exact scenario select one registered correction mode: non-economic amendment, reverse-and-replace, forward adjustment, source/physical reversal, reclassification or integration-only correction.

### How are economic identity and double count controlled?

New signed occurrences retain immutable occurrence identity, economic lineage, conservation group and metric contribution disposition. Current position and effective/recorded period flows are derived separately.

### How is closed-period meaning preserved?

Original effective and recorded times remain. The correction binds its permitted effective/recorded period under the closed-period policy; no silent back-edit occurs.

### Does ERP posting/payment change automatically?

No. Accounting-posted and paid facts remain external-authoritative actual families and require their own bounded correction/reconciliation.

### What happens to reports?

Recalculation produces new executions/results. If the declared use/materiality policy requires, a RestatementRecord creates a new immutable report snapshot/artifact and supersession/notice. The original issue remains historically intact.

### Can the old report support a new award?

Only after a current SubsequentRelianceAssessment checks restatement, access, evidence, conformance, definition support and intended use.

# 6. Blind execution D — GT-20 optional AI with incomplete context

## Given

A tenant enables an RFQ-analysis capability. A mandatory source is inaccessible; the tenant lowers token budget; a smaller provider model is available; an AI proposal was generated before a recipient/schema change.

## Questions and controlling answers

### Who defines the capability, prompts, tools, context/evaluation/resource policies and authority ceiling?

The product `AICapabilityRegistry`. Tenant configuration can only narrow and must pass `ConfigurationMonotonicityCheck` at enablement and every material change.

### Can tenant budget make the source optional?

No. Mandatory-source/context meaning is product-owned. If the resource floor cannot fit, allowed outcomes are queue, separately evaluated smaller profile, explicit task-scope narrowing, permitted subset/range, abstention or AI-off.

### Can the smaller provider inherit evaluation?

No. Every provider/model profile carries its own exact compatibility and current `SUFFICIENT_PASS`.

### What is the context result?

Missing mandatory source produces access-restricted/unknown-gap/blocked context—not `CONTEXT_COMPLETE`. Citations do not cure incomplete population.

### May the AI claim “all bids” or “current total”?

No. Those claims require complete underlying product population/time/access proof.

### Can the proposal execute after recipient/schema change?

No. Capability transition disposition and ProposalFreshnessPolicy mark it stale/invalid. Expected-version/confirmation guards are a second defense.

### May an agent issue the RFQ?

Only at L5 after the exact canonical command digest was confirmed by the same acting/represented principal and remains unchanged. The AI cannot create a tool, raise authority, self-accept or continue through effect indeterminacy.

### What happens when AI is disabled/unavailable?

The same conventional deterministic A0–A3 workflow remains complete.
