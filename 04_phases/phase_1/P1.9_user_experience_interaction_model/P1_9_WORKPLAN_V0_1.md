# 1. Objective

Close deterministic interaction meaning for internal users, suppliers/subcontractors, reviewers/auditors and later chat surfaces without choosing frontend technology or allowing the interface to become an authority, workflow engine, supplier network, CDE, BI platform or AI subsystem.

> **The interface may simplify interaction, but it may never simplify away authority, evidence, uncertainty, population, decision-use or correction meaning.**

P1.9 will be executed as one compressed but gated semantic cycle ending at a self-contained Claude hostile-audit packet. P1.9 does not close until external PASS and final checkpoint.

---

# 2. Mandatory inherited controls

- every state-changing action maps to one P1.7 registered COMMAND or ASYNC_OPERATION;
- QUERY, PROPOSAL, COMMAND and established effect remain visibly distinct;
- current authority, represented principal, scope, evidence and guards are checked for every new action;
- AwardDecision remains distinct from Commitment;
- upload remains distinct from accepted evidence/source fact;
- source submission, normalized representation, buyer adjustment and supplier-confirmed basis remain distinct;
- issue/send/delivery/read/ack/domain effect remain distinct;
- subset, range, restricted, stale, mixed-time, evidence-limited, indeterminate and restated results cannot be visually upgraded;
- conventional A0–A3 interaction remains complete without connectors, supplier accounts, chat or AI;
- P07 remains sole independent XL;
- product code remains locked.

# 3. Execution sequence

## Stage 1 — control and evidence

1. freeze P1.9 terminology, threats, evidence hierarchy and assumptions;
2. collect only targeted official external-party, high-consequence interaction, recovery, accessibility and RTL evidence;
3. reconcile ADR-0016 without presuming portal parity or email-only operation.

## Stage 2 — interaction grammar

4. freeze operation affordance, preview, authority disclosure, evidence requirement, validation, confirmation, submission and result semantics;
5. freeze accepted-versus-effect-established, idempotent recovery, unknown effect, cancellation, correction and batch behavior;
6. prohibit hidden commands, navigation-triggered commands and generic confirmation click-through.

## Stage 3 — task/context model

7. freeze work context and navigation without a universal procurement case root;
8. freeze task, history, evidence, report and control-observation linking across polycentric identities;
9. define internal A0–A3 task surfaces and optional P07 surfaces.

## Stage 4 — approval and irreversible action

10. freeze DOA, delegation, on-behalf-of, review, return, request-information, rejection, expiry and escalation interaction semantics;
11. freeze high-consequence preview and confirmation requirements;
12. preserve approval outcome versus domain command/effect.

## Stage 5 — external participation / ADR-0016

13. decide the bounded hybrid participation model;
14. freeze secure task-link, email/file, buyer-on-behalf and optional persistent-account modes;
15. freeze grant identity, forwarding/delegation, shared-mailbox, revision, addendum, acknowledgment, confidentiality and fallback semantics;
16. keep supplier relationship tenant-private and network membership optional.

## Stage 6 — evidence, communication and reporting

17. freeze source/version/location/reliance interaction;
18. freeze exact issue/member/recipient/channel and communication occurrence visibility;
19. freeze report value-state, quality, limitation, issued/current/restated and subsequent-reliance interaction;
20. require presentation/export/chat parity for load-bearing limitations.

## Stage 7 — queues, error, recovery and accessibility

21. freeze control-observation queue semantics without GRC/case gravity;
22. freeze validation, authorization, conflict, stale-version, duplicate, partial-batch, interrupted and unknown-effect recovery;
23. freeze no-connector manual/file fallback;
24. freeze semantic accessibility, responsive/mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, status-message, localization, Arabic/RTL, timezone, number and currency obligations.

## Stage 8 — conventional UI/chat coexistence

25. freeze complete conventional equivalents for supported chat queries/actions;
26. keep chat optional and P1.10-owned reasoning/autonomy deferred;
27. prohibit session memory, conversational wording or AI confidence from becoming authority.

## Stage 9 — golden thread and audit

28. execute the A0–A3 interaction golden thread through success, correction and failure paths;
29. assemble integrated candidate;
30. run internal hostile audit;
31. remediate every semantic blocker;
32. rerun hostile audit and regression/XL checks;
33. create self-contained Claude packet and exact prompt;
34. update canonical state to Claude pending only.

# 6. Closure gates

G1 — every state-changing interaction maps to one registered bounded operation with visible authority, evidence, guards, consequences and result.

G2 — query, draft/proposal, command acceptance and effect establishment remain operationally distinct.

G3 — navigation, task lists, work queues and history do not become business roots or truth writers.

G4 — approval, delegation, represented principal and high-consequence actions are explicit and cannot directly write domain truth.

G5 — source, normalized, evaluated and issued evidence layers remain distinct.

G6 — issue/send/delivery/read/ack/domain-effect distinctions remain visible.

G7 — subset/range/quality/restriction/indeterminacy/restatement limitations cannot be visually, conversationally or through export laundered.

G8 — issued/current/restated and issue-time/current-reliance distinctions are usable.

G9 — external participation is low-friction, tenant-private, optional-account and no-network-required.

G10 — external grants, recipient identity, forwarding, buyer-on-behalf and revision attribution are safe.

G11 — work queues/control observations do not become workflow/GRC/case truth.

G12 — validation, conflict, stale version, duplicate, partial batch and unknown-effect recovery are safe.

G13 — accessibility, mobile/responsive, status feedback, localization and RTL obligations are semantic and testable.

G14 — conventional A0–A3 surfaces are complete without connectors, chat or AI.

G15 — no second XL and no upstream regression.

G16 — internal hostile PASS and Claude packet complete.
