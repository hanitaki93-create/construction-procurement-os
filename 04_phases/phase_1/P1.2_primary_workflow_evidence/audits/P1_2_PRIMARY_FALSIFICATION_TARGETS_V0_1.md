# P1.2 Primary Falsification Targets v0.1

**Status:** BINDING BEFORE PRIMARY CAPTURE / NOT AN INTERVIEW SCRIPT  
**Purpose:** predeclare what observations would overturn the current provisional architecture.

## Use rule

Primary capture remains blind/verbatim first.

Do not expose these candidate claims to interviewees before capture.

After capture:
1. reconstruct the observed workflow in the contractor's own terms;
2. identify sources of truth, artifacts, approvals, workarounds and contradictions;
3. only then test the observation against these falsification targets;
4. a genuine primary falsifier reopens the relevant architecture even after Review A/B/C PASS.

## FT-01 — prior requirement authority

**Claim:** every sourcing route can reconcile to effective authorized requirement scope before award/commitment.

**Falsifier:** repeated independent cases show legitimate procurement/award proceeds with no prior requirement authority and the organization does not later reconstruct/reconcile one.

## FT-02 — allocation before commitment

**Claim:** commitment binds existing authorized allocation rather than creating scope authority itself.

**Falsifier:** primary systems/processes use commitment as the first/only authoritative scope allocation with no upstream conservation concept and no material duplicate/uncontrolled-procurement failure.

## FT-03 — award distinct from commitment

**Claim:** internal AwardDecision is distinct from the event that creates authoritative contractual commitment.

**Falsifier:** across independent contractors, the formal award action itself creates the obligation with no meaningful later formation/effectiveness distinction.

## FT-04 — claim / assessment / certification separation

**Claim:** supplier claim, buyer/QS assessment and certification are separately reconstructable truths where progress valuation exists.

**Falsifier:** independent contractor workflows consistently collapse them into one authoritative artifact/state without losing dispute, audit or economic meaning.

## FT-05 — composable fulfillment

**Claim:** receipt, progress valuation, milestone certification, service and deliverable mechanisms may compose by scope/economic component rather than being mutually exclusive by commitment type.

**Falsifier:** primary evidence shows one mutually exclusive fulfillment mode is determined by commitment type and material mixed cases do not occur.

## FT-06 — remeasurement still conserves scope

**Claim:** remeasurable work remains bounded by explicit authorized scope partition/cap while measured quantity may vary.

**Falsifier:** legitimate measured growth routinely occurs without any identifiable bounded scope partition, cap or governed change authority and does not create control loss.

## FT-07 — buyer recovery separation

**Claim:** LD, backcharge, defect/termination recovery and similar buyer entitlements are distinct from supplier-agreed contract change and gross certified earned value.

**Falsifier:** independent contract/process evidence shows these recoveries are universally and correctly represented as contract-value changes or certification reductions with no loss of legal/economic truth.

## FT-08 — commercial truth with external accounting authority

**Claim:** the procurement/commercial system can own contractual/commercial truth while AP/payment/job-cost accounting remains external through explicit authority/reconciliation.

**Falsifier:** independent deployments require the procurement product to own accounting posting/balance truth in order to maintain coherent procurement/commercial control.

## FT-09 — rectification procurement reconciliation

**Claim:** replacement/rectification procurement either restores source allocation capacity through valid reversal/release or requires governed basis expansion/additional authorized capacity before the replacement commitment consumes scope.

**Falsifier:** independent contractor workflows routinely procure rectification/replacement scope with no reconciliation to prior authorized scope, no returned capacity, no additional authorization, and no material control loss.

## FT-10 — exclusive-scope uniqueness

**Claim:** one active authorized requirement basis per exclusive declared physical/business scope is workable in practice, except explicit governed shared/joint responsibility.

**Falsifier:** independent contractors routinely maintain multiple legitimate parallel procurement authorities over the same exclusive physical/business scope without controlled partition/shared responsibility and without duplicate-procurement/control failure.

## Result states

Each target after evidence review must be marked:
- `SUPPORTED_PRIMARY`;
- `CONTRADICTED_PRIMARY`;
- `MIXED / VARIANT`;
- `NOT TESTED`;
- `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`.

Do not force mixed evidence into PASS/FAIL.

## Architecture consequence

`CONTRADICTED_PRIMARY` reopens the relevant candidate model/ADR even when earlier secondary-reference architecture reviews passed.
