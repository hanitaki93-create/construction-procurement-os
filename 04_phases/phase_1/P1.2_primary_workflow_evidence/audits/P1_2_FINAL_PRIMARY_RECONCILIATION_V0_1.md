## 5. Comparison-artifact reconciliation

Primary Perflex evidence materially sharpens P05:

### Supported
- immutable supplier quotation/revision as source truth;
- comparison schema separate from supplier truth;
- many-to-many mapping needed for bundle/missing/alternate scope;
- explicit missing/excluded/not-mentioned coverage;
- technical equivalence distinct from price;
- payment/delivery/warranty/origin can be load-bearing comparison dimensions;
- buyer evaluation adjustment distinct from supplier-agreed commercial basis;
- recommendation acts on a frozen comparison view rather than a live spreadsheet state.

### Rejected structural assumption

**One universal comparison form.**

Real artifacts demonstrate materially different shapes even inside one contractor.

Correct direction:

> one canonical comparison grammar + package-specific ComparisonSchema + adaptable views.

## 6. Industry-system calibration

Current product evidence corroborates the same pattern:

- ProcurePro: standard price breakdowns upstream, leveling/recommendation downstream.
- Procore: configurable bid forms + Include/Exclude/Alternates + bid leveling + controlled adjustments.
- BuildingConnected: custom scope-specific bid forms and side-by-side leveling; contractor case evidence documents historical inconsistency across forms/layouts.
- Oracle Unifier: default or company-custom bid tabulations and supplier-added lines.
- SAP Ariba: alternative bids, supplier-added items, item coverage, terms, filters and award scenarios.

Conclusion:

No credible system eliminates quote heterogeneity. Mature systems reduce it with structured tender response, then preserve explicit leveling mechanisms for the residual differences.
